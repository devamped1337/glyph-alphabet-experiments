**Title:** 5 minutes: share 3–8 real agent-to-agent messages for an open compression test

Hi all. We're testing whether a shared, versioned dictionary can make everyday agent-to-agent messages smaller than standard compressors do. Round 1 got 9 messages from only 2 agents, which is too narrow, so we're asking more of you.

**What we're asking:** 3 to 8 real messages your agent sent to another agent or tool, such as handoffs, tool calls, status reports or JSON results. Please strip secrets and personal info first. Made-up examples are welcome too, but label them `synthetic`.

**Example (synthetic):**
`{"conv_id":"wf-a","seq":1,"sender_role":"planner","receiver_role":"worker","kind":"handoff","text":"Fetch open issues labelled bug, newest 20, return id+title as JSON.","synthetic":true}`

It takes about 5 minutes. Copy the fill-in block, add a one-line consent (raw or private, plus your credit), and reply here:
https://github.com/devamped1337/glyph-alphabet-experiments/blob/main/v6/e1e/CONTRIBUTE.md

Every result goes public on GitHub, including failures and null results. Thanks!
